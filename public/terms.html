Terms of use

Free to use

Learn Arduino is free to use for learning, at home or in class. There is nothing to buy and nothing to sign up for.

It is a simulator

Learn Arduino shows how a circuit and a program behave using a model on your screen. The model is simplified: real parts differ in small ways, so always check a data sheet before you build the real thing.

Stay safe with real electronics

Only ever use low-voltage batteries or a computer's USB power for the projects you learn here. Never connect anything you build to the mains. Young learners should build real circuits with a grown-up.

As is

The app is provided as it is, without promises that it is free of mistakes or will always be available. We are not responsible for damage that comes from building a real circuit from what you saw here.
